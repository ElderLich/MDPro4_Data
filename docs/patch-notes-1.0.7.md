# MDPro4 1.0.7 translation notes

These companion catalog changes match the focused game commits.

- Progression AI can rebuild its deck from newly earned cards before each duel, or use a deck you assign from its own series collection. Choose the mode under Opponent Deck.

- Progression guests can choose their confirmed prizes and share the choice back to the organizer. Imports retain the saved player role and deck, while conflicting or repeated rewards are rejected. Sharing help now explains clipboard and file exports.

- Saved Progression Series now provides a confirmed Delete action for individual series. Other saves remain available, and deleting the active series safely clears its selection.
