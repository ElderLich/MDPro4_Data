# Combo Sheet examples

`Examples/` contains two offline teaching sheets in the same bounded schema as
community submissions. They are available without an account. Copy an example
to a local draft to edit it; opening or copying never publishes it.

- **Chimera — starter routes** covers all ten source combos as fifteen explicit
  variants and 196 instructional steps across **Mirror** (four variants),
  **Apprentice** (three) and **Gazelle** (eight), based on
  [Drident's Chimera sheet](https://www.masterduelmeta.com/spreadsheet/6a269450d654689b59c9d8ac).
- **Chaos Ritual — complete routes and branches** covers all nine source combos
  and nineteen nested branch entries in nine guides and 143 instructional steps.
  Its exact tabs are **Pure Chaos** (one guide), **Branded Chaos (Best Variant)**
  (three), **DM Chaos** (one) and **Turn 0 combos** (four), based on
  [Wahaby's Chaos Ritual sheet](https://www.masterduelmeta.com/spreadsheet/6a2352210d46d336afd56f52).
  This is the four-tab sheet visible in the supplied screenshot, located through
  the linked Master Duel Meta spreadsheet catalog.

The two adapted spreadsheet examples contain **24 guides/variants and 339 instructional steps**,
independently written from the observed procedures. Chimera's source has no
structured nested branches; its inline alternatives become explicit variants.
Chaos branches remain conditional steps within their owning guide, including
three empty source alternatives represented by the base continuation. Follow the
branch whose prerequisites apply rather than combining every displayed endpoint.

Every guide now has an explicit starting-hand/requirement diagram and an
**Endboard and follow-up** diagram. The two sheets also include **330 step
diagrams**: all 196 Chimera steps and 134 Chaos steps. The remaining nine Chaos
entries explain branch decisions, restrictions or matching-type references and
retain their complete instructions and inspectable card lists. No steps or
variants were removed.

The original **Enneacraft - a practical flip-control course** was moved to the
author's local **My combo sheets** library at their request. Its 17 guides and
78 steps are no longer bundled in Examples. A Tools-only content fixture and
[native-core receipt](../../Tools/Online/enneacraft-course-20261021.md) retain the
validation history; the author can choose when to publish their local sheet.

These diagrams use authored card occurrences, connectors, actions and locations.
A card can appear twice to show, for example, Phara being discarded by an effect
and then summoned from the GY. Named groups separate independent requirements:
the protected Mind Shuffle opening is **(White Dragon OR Ecclesia) + (Griffoh OR
Black Chaos)**. End diagrams distinguish established field cards, hand/GY
resources, alternative routes and conditional later summons. An arrow describes
the annotated sequence; it does not make the guide an executable replay.

Source links remain in documentation; each sheet keeps author credit and omits
`sourceUrl`. No website artwork, HTML, comments, account information or vote totals
are imported. The prose remains authored English content, like a user's
submission; editor controls and card inspection use game locales. A durable
[coverage receipt](../../Tools/Online/combo-examples-20261021.md) maps all nineteen
source combo identities and every nested branch to the shipped instructions.

Card IDs resolve against the installed card database. Some reference names have
changed: Ritual of Light and Darkness is **Light and Darkness Ritual**, Chaos
Hats is **Chaos Magical Hats**, Mystic Box of Chaos is **Chaos Mystic Box**,
Mystical Celtic Sage is **Celtic Mystic**, Corrupted Ritual Records is **Ragged
Records of Rites**, Astrolasma Urania is **Astrazma Urania**, and the referenced
ultimate magical swordsman is **Black Chaos**. The Phara example uses **Phara the
Primordial Goddess**. In-game card tiles show the installed localized name.

Starting-hand notes describe additional generic materials/discards. Endboard
annotations distinguish field cards, cards in hand/GY and opponent-turn
follow-up. A generic card ID of `0` represents an explained discard or material,
not a missing installed card. Notes retain relevant costs, timing, linked zones
and restrictions, and correct source ambiguities in material counts and card
locations. Schema/card/script checks and production example loading do not prove
native duel execution. These guides still need human duel validation against the
selected rules/banlist and interruption timing. The Enneacraft receipt records
the smaller set of exact native-core scenarios that have now passed; it does not
claim exhaustive execution of every guide or interruption. Future revisions must preserve
the distinction between conditional follow-up and an established board.
