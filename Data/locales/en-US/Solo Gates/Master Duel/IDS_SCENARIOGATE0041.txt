Legend of the Starry Dragon
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1001]
One starry night.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1002]
Some spend time with their family members,
others spend time with their loved ones
and pray for another day of peace and wellness.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1003]
On this night when the world is full of happiness,
a certain ancient legend is told:
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1004]
The Legend of the Starry Dragon.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1005]
A holy dragon appears on a beautiful starry night
with a knight in a white robe on its back.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1006]
The "good" are given a blessing and guidance,
while the "wicked" are thrown into holy fire
that burns away evil.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1007]
The tale is passed down from parent to child.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1008]
It transmits the parent's wish that their child
lives a pure and righteous life, so that they
may one day receive blessings from the holy dragon.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1009]
This is why it has been told
from generation to generation.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1010]
For the uninformed it is a mere fairy tale.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT1011]
However, there is a hidden truth to this story.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2001]
A truth hidden within the legend.
That is the battle between the dragon
and the "evil" beings.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2002]
Since the beginning of time, there have
been demonic beings that spread disorder.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2003]
Many people were suffering because
of these demons' mischief.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2004]
The angels that watch over the people
took pity and prayed to heaven.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2005]
In answer to that holy prayer,
"<card mrk='15870' get='name'/>" descended upon the Earth.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2006]
The Starry Dragon unleashed its might
to save humankind.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2007]
The angels took arms as knights obedient to the will
of the dragon and served to purge the evil.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2008]
In shining platinum armor, the white-winged angels
soared through the starry sky.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2009]
These angels were called the "Starry Knights."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2010]
Many of the demons were purged by
the dragon and the angels before they
were even witnessed by humans.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2011]
No matter how much time passes,
people will remain ignorant of the
truth while they pass on the legend.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2012]
The traces of the holy dragon and the angels
silently disappear as they fulfill their duties.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0041.TXT2013]
To the twinkling stars flowing like the Milky Way
we give our thanks and holy blessings.
