The Fairies Who Paint the Weather
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1001]
A little girl gazed out the window
at the scenery outside as it rained.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1002]
Watching the girl, her mother called to her.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1003]
“You don't like rain, do you? ”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1004]
“No, I don't.
It's boring when it rains, because I can't play outside. ”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1005]
The girl replied without turning around,
but her voice betrayed her sour mood.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1006]
“...Mom, why does it rain? ”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1007]
The mother replied to the child, whose inquiry was so direct.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1008]
“It rains because
the weather fairies are working
on behalf of all of us. ”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1009]
Perhaps due to the unexpected answer, 
the girl now turned to her mother 
with an intrigued expression.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1010]
Her mother began to speak.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1011]
“There are weather fairies in the sky 
who use magical paint, brushes, and crayons, 
to deftly draw pictures in the sky.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1012]
"The pictures turn into weather,
delivering blessings to all."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1013]
Seemingly fond of the expression 
“drawing pictures in the sky,”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT1014]
the little girl left the window
to sit on her mother's lap on the sofa,
where she waited for her to continue the story.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2001]
As she gazed out the window, the mother continued her story.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2002]
Rain is depicted by “The Weather Painter Rain.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2003]
She's a bit shy, but she's most diligent.
It rains so that
trees and flowers around the world may be nourished.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2004]
Depicting the snow is “The Weather Painter Snow.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2005]
She's a bright, cheerful, hard-working maiden.
The snow this child makes
blankets the villages and mountains white in winter.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2006]
Depicting the clouds is “The Weather Painter Cloud,”
a handsome young man.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2007]
Sometimes he's in his own world,
but with a magic marker he can draw any cloud imaginable.
You must have seen clouds that look exactly 
like other objects, right?
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2008]
It is the job of “The Weather Painter Sun” to draw sunshine.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2009]
An old man with a fluffy beard,
he kindly watches over the others.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2010]
On laundry days
it's most helpful when he decides to draw sunshine.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2011]
Lightning is etched by “The Weather Painter Thunder.”
A boy who's a bit of a prankster.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2012]
He sketches lightning in order to surprise everyone,
and sometimes his overly zealous artwork
shocks even himself.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2013]
The one who draws the sky curtain Aurora
is “The Weather Painter Aurora.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2014]
The Aurora she paints are of such beauty,
I hope one day we can glimpse them together.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2015]
“The Weather Painter Rainbow” depicts rainbows.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2016]
The ringleader.
Using seven colors of paint,
she builds a rainbow bridge in the sky.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2017]
Not only are rainbows beautiful,
but treasures lie at their feet...
Or so the story goes.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2018]
After hearing the story,
the little girl exhaled with satisfaction,
as if she'd finally remembered to breathe.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2019]
“There are so many fairies in the sky! 
You know so much, Mother, but have you ever met one?”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2020]
Her mother smiles gently,
and stroking her daughter's head, replies,
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2021]
“Yes, when I was about your age.
If you're a good girl surely
you'll meet your own fairy one day.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0016.TXT2022]
—Perhaps among the people you've encountered,
there may be some that met a fairy.
