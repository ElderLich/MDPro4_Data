[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1005]
A long time ago there was constant conflict between the nations.
The turmoil was the very picture of hell on earth.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1006]
In the midst of that era, in order to quell the strife,
one feudal lord assembled the greatest Karakuri craftsmen.
The feudal lord proclaimed to the engineers:
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1007]
“I want you to develop soldiers stronger than firearms.
This is an important command.
I want you to give your all.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1008]
Immediately the engineers set out to develop
 devices capable of wielding great power.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1009]
Crucial to the devices
were Golden Gears located in a faraway country.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1010]
The feudal lord granted the engineers' wishes,
successfully acquiring the Golden Gears.
And thus, mysterious devices of untold power were forged.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1011]
After several years, “Karakuri” equipped with 
the Golden Gears were completed.
The workmanship of the “Karakuri” was unrivaled.
Their handiwork was impressive to all who beheld it.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1012]
Fortifications included “Karakuri Castle,” 
a cornerstone of armor and defense.
They were now prepared for battle.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1013]
Under the command of “Karakuri Shogun mdl 00 ‘Burei',”
the Karakuri soldiers went headlong into battle.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1014]
After so many battles,
what a blessing that an era of calm had been ushered in.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT1015]
The era of war had come to an end.
Now we'll explore what lie in store next for the Karakuri.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2001]
So in times of peace,
what became of the Karakuri?
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2002]
Delving yet again into the tome “Karakuri Anatomy”,
next we'll examine how the Karakuri lived.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2003]
Since “Karakuri Shogun mdl 00 ‘Burei'” had become ruler,
many years had passed.
Around Karakuri Castle there was unparalleled prosperity.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2004]
On the highroads not only Karakuri,
but townsfolk, such as zealous merchants, were common sights.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2005]
Merchants peddled
“Karakuri Gama Oil” and “Karakuri Gold Dust” on the street,
wares that were medicine for the Karakuri.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2006]
These were in high demand, and for the merchants
they proved most profitable.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2007]
The fortress in which the Karakuri live is long unchanged.
The Gears that keep it functioning work 
as well as the water wheels by the river.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2008]
The Karakuri live a peaceful life,
though there is one thing alone that they fear.
That thing is fire.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2009]
When a fire starts in town, even if it isn't in an area of great concern,
the Karakuri can help each other extinguish it.
Because the Karakuri live by lending each other a hand.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2010]
The Karakuri exist to this day.
That much we know from the many books that remain.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0011.TXT2011]
As this story continues to unravel, new discoveries await
around every corner.
