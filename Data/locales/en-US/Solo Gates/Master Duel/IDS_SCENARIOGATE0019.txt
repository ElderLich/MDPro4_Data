[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TITLE1001]
The Herald's Guidance
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1001]
The rule of the gods had ended,
and humans had ruled the earth for many ages.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1002]
Though many gods had fled,
many people continued to believe that the gods
watched over the world, even as their visages changed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1003]
In a remote domain on a distant continent,
gods had long been worshipped by the people living there.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1004]
It was a God that spread its wings
and emitted a blinding light from their entire body:
The “Herald.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1005]
Among those living there, a few have seen
or even heard the voice of the “Herald.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1006]
Though their depictions differed,
they insisted they'd seen the “Herald.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1007]
The atmosphere about it and its afterglow
was precisely that of an angel
they'd pictured in their minds, they told.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1008]
And according to those who'd heard its voice,
it hadn't actually made a sound.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1009]
The meaning of the words came not through their ears
but directly into their minds, they told.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1010]
And when asked if they'd seen or heard
the “Herald,”
their replies were eerily consistent.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1011]
It was always in moments of confession that it appeared.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1012]
All assert they fainted soon after
but awoke with a comforting feeling of “forgiveness”
throughout their bodies.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1013]
Some find it hard to believe
these people's stories.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT1014]
Nonetheless, the “Herald”
is worshipped by many people.

[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2001]
In the frontier, the “Herald”
is enshrined in a temple.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2002]
As for when this temple was constructed,
nobody knows.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2003]
It is old but well maintained
and has a sacred, solemn atmosphere about it.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2004]
However, depending on who you ask, that structure
may have an unusual aura about it.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2005]
It appears not to have been built as a place of worship,
but to contain something,
merely covered up as a “shrine.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2006]
The central part is hidden,
and it is said there are gods' statues inside.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2007]
And, into that inner sanctum,
only a select few priests, or “Diviners,” are allowed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2008]
The “Diviner of the Herald”:
The person who receives the oracle from the “Herald.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2009]
She claims that from a certain age,
she began to hear the “voice.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2010]
After many trials and tests,
a priest determined that the voice she heard
belonged to the “Herald.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2011]
This “Diviner” is not one who commands the people.
Her role is to correctly decipher the oracles for people
and protecting them from natural disasters.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2012]
Her resolute power and connection with people
had helped pass along to future generations
her role of “Diviner.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0019.TXT2013]
And one girl
will continue as the new “Diviner”
to connect people with the gods.
