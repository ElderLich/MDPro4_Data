[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_ABOUTLINK_B]
A Monster Zone ahead of a Link Arrow is simply referred to as a zone that a Link Monster is "pointing to." You may encounter various card effects that allow you to Special Summon a monster to a zone that a Link Monster is pointing to. 
If a Link Monster is pointing to another monster, then the two monsters are "linked." If two Link Monsters are pointing to each other, that makes them both "co-linked." 
You may unlock a variety of effects for yourself when you link or co-link monsters.
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_ABOUTLINK_H]
Learn the Link Monster lingo
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_ADVANCE_B]
This method is used for Summoning Monsters Level 5 and above.
 You must Tribute a certain amount of Monsters on your side of the Field in order to perform this Summon. The amount will depend on the Level of the Monster you want to Tribute Summon. (Setting Monsters Level 5 and above will also require a Tribute(s).)
 As you only have one Normal Summon per turn, you will not be able to Tribute Summon if you have already performed your Normal Summon for the turn.
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_ADVANCE_H]
What are Tribute Summons?
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_ATKDEFCALC_B]
There are many cards with effects that can manipulate a monster's ATK and/or DEF. 
Some cards may raise or lower ATK/DEF by a set amount, while others may halve it, double it, or make it 0. 
They can be very useful to have in your arsenal!
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_ATKDEFCALC_H]
Effects that change ATK and DEF
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_BATTLEDAMAGE_B]
The position the Monster is in will determine the value or number that is used in battle calculations.
 1) Attack Position VS Attack Position - The ATK of both Monsters will be used.
 2) Attack Position VS Defense Position - The ATK of the Monster in Attack Position and the DEF of the Monster in Defense Position will be used.
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_BATTLEDAMAGE_H]
How is battle damage calculated?
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_BATTLEPHASE_B]
The Battle Phase is further split into four parts, called "steps": the "Start Step", "Battle Step", "Damage Step", and "End Step". 
The "Battle Step" is the part where preparations for battle happen. During this step, players may declare attacks with their monsters and select their target(s) for attack. Card effects that activate upon attack declaration, or when a monster is targeted for an attack, can also be used.
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_BATTLEPHASE_H]
The different parts of the Battle Phase
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_CHAINORDER_B]
"Chains" are built from cards being activated in response to other cards. "Spell Speed" is an important aspect to understand how "Chains" work. 
When multiple cards with the same Spell Speed are activated, they will be chained in this order: 
Example 1: Chain 1 - Mandatory effects → Chain 2 - Optional effects
Example 2: Chain 1 - Card effects of face-up cards (on the field or in the Graveyard, etc.) → Chain 2 - Card effects of face-down cards (from the hand or Set cards, etc.)
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_CHAINORDER_H]
How a Chain is built
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_CHAIN_B]
A Chain is created when you activate the Effect of a Card in response to the activation of another Card Effect.
 For example, there are many Counter Trap Cards that you can activate and Chain onto a Card activation or Effect in order to negate it.
 A list of the Cards that you can Chain will be displayed when it is possible for you to Chain a card. Then, you can choose whether or not you want to Chain a Card.
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_CHAIN_H]
What are Chains?
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_CONDITIONSS_B]
There are many monsters with effects that allow them to be Special Summoned once their conditions have been met, called "Special Summon Monsters." 
There are Special Summon Monsters besides Ritual, Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, and Link Monsters.
If you want to Special Summon them from the Graveyard through effects like that of "<card mrk='4842'/>," they must have been correctly Special Summoned to the field before they were sent to the Graveyard.
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_CONDITIONSS_H]
Special Summoning monsters
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_DAMAGESTEP_B]
Once you've finished preparing for battle in the "Battle Step," you will then move to the "Damage Step."
Most cards cannot be activated during the Damage Step, but there are a few exceptions. 
Effects that can be activated during the Damage Step mostly involve changing ATK/DEF and negating card activations.
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_DAMAGESTEP_H]
Cards that can be activated during the Damage Step
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_DAMAGETYPE_B]
These are the two types of damage that can be inflicted. 
"Battle damage" is inflicted when two monsters battle or when a monster directly attacks a player. "Effect damage" is dealt via card effects. 
When you're finding it difficult to inflict "Battle damage", try and think of ways you can inflict "Effect damage" instead.
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_DAMAGETYPE_H]
Battle damage and Effect damage

[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_POSITION_B]
Monsters can be placed in 3 different battle positions.
 1) When a Monster is Summoned from the hand, it will be placed in face-up Attack Position. If you want to declare an attack with a Monster, it must be in this position.
 2) When you Set a Monster from the hand, it will be placed in face-down Defense Position.
 3) When a Monster in face-down Defense Position is attacked by an opponent, it will be changed to face-up Defense Position. Monsters may also be changed to face-up Defense Position via Card Effects.

This Summoning method allows you to bring out Xyz Monsters.
 You must first gather to the Field the number of Monsters listed in the Xyz Monster's description as materials. The Levels of each material must match the Xyz Monster's Rank in numerical value.
 Most Xyz Monsters require 2 Monsters of the same Level, but others may require 3 or more. Some may also require materials of specific Types and/or Attributes.
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_XYZ_01_H]
What are Xyz Summons?
[IDS_HELP.STRATEGY_XYZ_02_B]
During your Main Phase, you will need to Overlay 2 or more face-up Monsters of the same Level on your Field in the Zone you would like to Summon your Xyz Monster to. Then, you can Summon an Xyz Monster whose conditions have been met from the Extra Deck.

 The Monsters that have been stacked underneath the Xyz Monster become its materials, which you can use to activate its Effects.
