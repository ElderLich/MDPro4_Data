[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TITLE1001]
Dream Domination
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1001]
Our story takes place 
in the far reaches of a town somewhere...
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1002]
In a back alley hidden from the main street,
where the hustle and bustle could barely be heard,
a stranger wandered about in a trance-like state.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1003]
Then, a voice spoke from nowhere,
calling out to them, beckoning...
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1004]
"Say... This is a rather strange dream
you've landed up in, isn't it? "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1005]
And when the stranger turned,
there stood a fortune teller behind them.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1006]
The fortune teller curled a finger,
calling for the stranger
to come closer.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1007]
"Those who visit this town are all lost in a dream...
I have seen many souls with the same
look in their eyes as you. "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1008]
"Now, let me ask you...
What is it you see in your dreams? "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1009]
The stranger felt as though there was 
nothing they could hide.
Taken by the fortune teller's mysterious presence,
recounts of their dreams began to spill forth 
from the stranger's lips.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1010]
Once the stranger had finished,
the fortune teller broke their silence.

[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1011]
"They say there are two kinds of dreams—
good omens that bring the dreamer 
happiness and good fortune,
and bad omens that attract nothing 
but disaster and misfortune. "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1012]
"Some also say that all the dreams we see
are all dominated and being controlled
by a lone figure somewhere. "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1013]
"Perhaps the dreams you have seen
have also been given to you by this individual, "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1014]
the fortune teller said, 
not a hint of dishonesty in their voice.
The stranger swallowed, hanging on to every word.
The fortune teller continued as tension hung 
like a thick fog.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1015]
The one who dominates dreams changes its form 
depending on what dream it is giving to the dreamer.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1016]
In good dreams, it will look like an angel.
But in bad dreams, it will look like a demon.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1017]
The disciples of the dream ruler will appear
in the real world and leave a mysterious mirror
by the dreamer's bedside, drawing them into the dream world.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1018]
One could say that the mirror is a mystical gateway to the land of dreams.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1019]
The sun and moon motifs etched into the mirror
can change the world of the dream depending
on which one has risen and which one has set.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1020]
If the sun is above, 
the dreamer is blessed with a good omen.
But if the moon is the one on top, 
the dreamer is thrust into a nightmare.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT1021]
Which was it for you, I wonder...
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2001]
The fortune teller mused.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2002]
The dream world of the sun
was one filled with light and resplendence.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2003]
It was a beautiful land of free and spirited souls,
of holy beasts, gallant knights,
and frolicking maidens.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2004]
But on the other hand, the dream world of the moon
was one drowned in a deep and inky darkness.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2005]
It was a dreadful land 
where souls were trapped by despair,
home to demonic beasts and knights of doom,
and reapers of death who showed no mercy.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2006]
The dream ruler masterfully controls these worlds,
bringing into them countless people.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2007]
And in turn the dreams these people 
have become their power.
Through the dreams that the dream ruler gives people,
it is able to gain control of their hearts.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2008]
And those who become unable to tell dream from reality
are said to never wake again.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2009]
"What will become of me, then? "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2010]
The stranger asked, quivering with fright,
hoping the fortune teller would have an answer.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2011]
"There is nothing to fear, my friend. "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2012]
"Indeed, the tale of the two dream worlds
has been passed down through many generations,
and many people in our time still visit them. "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2013]
"Among the accounts from numerous dreamers, the most
fascinating is that of a certain group of children. "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2014]
"They did not become trapped in their dream, however,
and upon waking, they regaled their adventures
with great joy to anyone who would listen. "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2015]
"I'm sure that for you, your dreams
are a good source of power for you too. "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2016]
"Be not afraid and have faith in yourself.
That could be your greatest strength
against the dream ruler. "
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2017]
The stranger sighed, shoulders sagging with relief.
Now that they felt safer,
they left the fortune teller and the alleyway.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0024.TXT2018]
Well, what do you think of that, dear reader?
Perhaps your dream tonight will be one from the dream ruler too...
