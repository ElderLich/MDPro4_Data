[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TITLE1001]
The Pyroxene Warriors
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1001]
Pyroxene warriors
the “Gem-Knights,” with hearts of justice.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1002]
Diligent in their training
but reluctant to utilize their prowess in battle,
they led a peaceful existence.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1003]
However, those calm days were threatened
by the flame people, the “Laval.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1004]
This aggressive nation of flame warriors
sought to invade the land
of the “Gem-Knights.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1005]
Averse to this fruitless conflict,
the “Gem Knights” did not aggressively engage them.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1006]
But for the “Laval,”
the inaction of the “Gem-Knights” was fuel to the fire.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1007]
Day by day, collisions increased
between the “Laval” and the “Gem-Knights.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1008]
At last, the “Gem-Knights” resolved themselves to
a full-on conflict to protect their homeland.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1009]
The “Gem-Knights” possessed a “Core”
that shone like a gem, which they activated
as a force to aid them in battle.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1010]
In addition, they could utilize the “Core”
to fuse with other knights to become 
even more powerful warriors.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1011]
The fused “Gem-Knights” include the following.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1012]
“Gem-Knight Ruby,” who repelled the enemy.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1013]
“Gem-Knight Aquamarine,” who defended its companions.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT1014]
“Gem-Knight Topaz,” who tormented its opponents.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT2001]
At first, some of the “Gem-Knights”
laid in waiting for their enemies,
but eventually, they decided to join the fight, one by one.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT2002]
In the midst of fierce battle,
the “Gem-Knights” fused further into 
an even more powerful force.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT2003]
However, this clash between the tribes
was only the beginning of the turmoil that followed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT2004]
The “Gem-Knights,” who had risen to defend their comrades,
were now caught up in the great swell of battle.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT2005]
At first, they loathed the conflict,
but to counter that great evil, they eventually fused
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT2006]
with other races to grow more powerful,
such as the “Vylon,” who descended from heaven,
or even the “Laval” themselves.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT2007]
The “Gem-Knights”: the Pyroxene warriors
with hearts of justice.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0009.TXT2008]
Their fate shall unfold
in glorious detail in another tale.
