[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TITLE1001]
The Absolute Monarch
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1001]
In this world, some beings embodied
the concept of destruction.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1002]
Their dignified appearance and behavior
were like those of “Monarchs.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1003]
No one knew for what purpose the “Monarchs”
sought the destruction of the world.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1004]
They wield their paranormal powers
and reduce everything in their sight to nothingness.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1005]
Thunder booms before them,
and trees turn brittle.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1006]
They boil the mountains
and crack the earth.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1007]
They conjure storms
and submerge all into unfathomable darkness.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1008]
The world steeped in turmoil and rage, subsumed by violence.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1009]
And when there is nothing more to destroy,
they move to the next land,
searching for further destruction.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1010]
All that witness this great destruction
shudder in fear.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1011]
It is the fate of any unfortunate enough to be in their path
to disappear in an instant.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1012]
And—
among the destroyers of this world,
there is an Alpha.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1013]
Its overwhelming power of intimidation lays
other destroyers prone, and it possesses untold strength.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1014]
“Erebus, the Underworld Monarch”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT1015]
“Erebus, the Underworld Monarch”
sits on his throne in the darkest of darkness,
looking on at the destruction of the world
as if it were the most natural thing.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2001]
The advances of the Monarchs, 
who embody the very concept of destruction, continue.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2002]
How many countless worlds must they have destroyed?
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2003]
Lightning storms, blizzards, lava, and seismic catastrophy.
All manners of destruction assail the world.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2004]
As they advance, the Monarchs
grow ever stronger,
perpetually pushing the boundaries of destruction.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2005]
Their power defiles all matter equally.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2006]
There is no way to discern
whether or not the Monarchs are aware of what they destroy.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2007]
They advance, thoroughly
expunging all that enters their fields of vision,
no matter the scenery.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2008]
However, in a land reduced to rubble,
from the heavens descended a Creator.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2009]
“Ehther the Heavenly Monarch”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2010]
A savior descended, as if a miracle,
to a land awaiting its destruction.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2011]
Whether this Creator could be counted
as one of the Monarchs is unknown.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2012]
The ground in which the Creator has landed slowly
begins to revive.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2013]
A day will surely come when this land,
regenerated by the Creator, will be destroyed again.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0005.TXT2014]
Nonetheless, with no reward, “Ehther the Heavenly Monarch”
fills this ruined world with the light of rebirth.
