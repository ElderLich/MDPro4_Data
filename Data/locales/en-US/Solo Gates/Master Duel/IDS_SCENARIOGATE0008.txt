[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TITLE1001]
The Mystery of the Megalith
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1001]
In ancient times, 
many monumental stone structures were built.
Stone circles and pyramids
are some examples of this.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1002]
However, in many other worlds, there are
various types of colossal structures.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1003]
Let's take a look at one of these,
the grand stone structures known as
“The Megalith.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1004]
The “Megalith Ophiel”
is a white statue that resembles an angel.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1005]
Her wavy hair hangs down to her shoulders,
and her eyes are closed in a gentle expression.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1006]
What catches the eye most of all
are the wings on her back, like those of an angel.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1007]
Her radiant holiness inspires
reverence and modesty in all who behold her.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1008]
On the other hand is the black devil,
“Megalith Hagith.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1009]
A statue of a haggard devil,
crouching with contorted limbs.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1010]
Its face hides behind a mask;
the only clue at its expression
is it's slightly exposed mouth.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1011]
The thorns that are driven into the side of its body
inspire dread in the beholder.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1012]
The sculpture of two children, 
the white and black, “Megalith Och.”
They appear a lovely pair of twins.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1013]
By the shape of their wings,
the white sculpture appears to be an angel,
while the black sculpture appears to be a demon.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1014]
It's also notable that
they wear contrasting facial expressions.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1015]
The pedestals of the “Megalith”
are comprised of an unknown material, not of natural stone.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1016]
Their distinct features include a heptagonal decoration 
and a pattern imbued with mysticism.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT1017]
What kind of power is hidden within
these pedestals, which emit a gentle light?
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2001]
The pedestals of the “Megalith”
may act as a door to another world
or a stake connecting another creature.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2002]
Appearing from the pedestal of “Megalith Ophiel,”
“Megalith Phaleg” boasts two pairs of wings.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2003]
A far cry from the gentle “Ophiel,”
this ferocious angel is adorned with 
a helmet and shield, wielding a spear.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2004]
Appearing from the pedestal of “Megalith Hagith,”
the great devil “Megalith Bethor” has four wings.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2005]
It is a devil of formidable build
with glaring, golden horns.

[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2006]
From the pedestal of “Megalith Och”
blazes the many-headed dragon “Megalith Aratron.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2007]
Its momentum threatens to flatten the earth
and to swallow all.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2008]
It can be said all these comprise but one form
of the power born from the “Megalith.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2009]
But that is not all.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2010]
The “Megalith” have the power
to create a kind of force field even after destroyed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2011]
How were they made,
and what is the true identity of their hidden power?
The “Megalith” are entities shrouded in great mystery.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0008.TXT2012]
The key to solving this mystery
may be a truth that emerges
from beyond the “Megalith” themselves.
