[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TITLE1001]
The Steam Dinosaurs of Unchartered Lands
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1001]
A great river rushing with pristine water,
a forest inhabited with a bounty of creatures,
a volcano brimming with boiling lava...
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1002]
Those are unexplored lands full of the wonders of nature.
Therein creatures with their own unique ecology
live; mechanical dinosaurs.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1003]
Known as “Dinomists,” their novelty
lies in the steam engines
that power their hulking forms.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1004]
The key to their survival in that great wilderness
is that their bodies are suited
to the harsh environment.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1005]
This time let's take a closer look
at the shrouded ecology of the “Dinomists.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1006]
Drifting with the gentle flow of the river
is the long-necked “Dinomist Plesios.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1007]
It derives its energy from water 
absorbed through a pipe in its tail.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1008]
Not only is stabilizing energy produced
by its hover unit supporting the beast's own weight,
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1009]
the balance between the water imbibed
and the steam released prevents flooding of the river.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1010]
As a result, land-dwelling companions
are protected from potential flood damage.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1011]
Flying freely through the great skies
is the winged “Dinomist Pteran.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1012]
Turbines on both wings collect moisture in the air
and generate energy for high-speed flight.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1013]
The water vapor released is cooled by the atmosphere,
turning into rainclouds and hydrating the earth.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1014]
Among the known “Dinomists,”
those in the waters and skies are a rarity.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT1015]
Next let's take a look at the many “Dinomists”
that dwell on land.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2001]
This time we direct our gaze to the land.
Here a great many “Dinomists” can be observed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2002]
Raising a great dust as they trample the earth
are “Dinomist Ceratops” and
“Dinomist Stegosaur.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2003]
Hardly a competition,
theirs is closer to a patrol
that protects the territory.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2004]
“Ceratops” use sharp protrusions on their bodies
to crush the boulders spewed by volcanic eruptions,
making the land more suitable for their companions.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2005]
“Stegosaurs'” backs are lined with
 fins in the shapes of swords
 that monitor the surrounding climate.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2006]
Fluctuations in temperature and wind velocity
that pose potential danger to the land
are sensed by those fins.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2007]
In the unknown lands where the “Dinomists” live
there is one particularly perilous place.
Namely, the volcano belt.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2008]
Herein colossal creatures glare at each other,
vying to be the boss of all “Dinomists.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2009]
Those are the “Dinomist Spinos”
and “Dinomist Rex.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2010]
Both convert their steam engines' energy
into electricity, and their power
surpasses that of other “Dinomists.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2011]
The two are rivals,
but when a foreign enemy invades
they band together to protect their territory.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2012]
That is their role as bosses.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0014.TXT2013]
And so the “Dinomists”
perform their symbiotic roles
as they coexist in those unexplored lands.
