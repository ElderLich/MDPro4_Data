The Creatures of Naturia Forest
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1001]
On this planet, there is a woodland stretching out far over the horizon: Naturia Forest.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1002]
It is a vast, fertile region called home to bugs, plants, stones, and other creatures given life by the earth itself.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1003]
There are no wars here, and rarely are there any disturbances, save for the occasional human wandering in.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1004]
The forest's inhabitants are peaceful in nature and always try to avoid conflict.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1005]
The Naturia have reshaped the vegetation to block passage through the forest, and the rock Naturia have closed off the dangerous cliffs and holes to create a path.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1006]
In order to lead the wandering humans away from the heart of the forest, and prevent them from going deeper into the forest.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1007]
Now and again, when an innocent child strays in, the Naturia themselves will appear to guide the way back home.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1008]
However, not all who stray into the forest are pure in their intentions.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1009]
Some scoundrels are scheming to steal away a part of the forest Keeper's body.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1010]
"It is said the power within it would raise a man's strength unto that of a god."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1011]
"It is said to be an ingredient for a miracle elixir that grants eternal life and cures all disease."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1012]
"It is said the sum it would fetch would be too great to spend even in three lifetimes."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1013]
Having placed their faith in these dubious whisperings, they have trespassed deep into the forest.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1014]
The inhabitants of the forest show no mercy to intruders with such ignoble aims.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1015]
Even the nonviolent Naturia have their ways of roughing up intruders and encouraging them to go back whence they came.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1016]
That said, the Naturia do not wish to maim or kill anybody.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1017]
For instance, the parched intruders reached out to take a bite of "Naturia Strawberry"...
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1018]
But hidden in the fruit's shadow is the prick of "Naturia Horneedle"!
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1019]
The intruders fled in surprise, only to be caught in the web of "Naturia Spiderfang"!
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1020]
Dangling helplessly, they then receive a tackle from "Naturia Bamboo Shoot"!
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1021]
How the intruders fled with all their might, rubbing their bruises and licking their wounds!
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1022]
Many of the Naturia are small in stature and equally slight in strength.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1023]
But by cooperating as neighbors, they help each other to protect the forest from their enemies.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1024]
Things continued in this manner for hundreds upon thousands of years, the forest blessed with abundance and united in peace.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1025]
However, eventually, a new menace appeared that threatened that very way of life.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT1026]
These invaders from outer space are known as Worms.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT2001]
With the arrival of the Worms, the era of peace in Naturia Forest was ruptured at last.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT2002]
At first, they seemed to be no more than a minor curiosity, like others that appeared in the forest from time to time.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT2003]
And so they were dealt with in the same way as any other interlopers.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT2004]

[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT2005]
"The entire forest will be overrun if things continue like this."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT2006]
A number of the Naturia stood up, vowing to take down the Worms and protect the forest.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT2007]
These were the Keepers, the most powerful beings in Naturia Forest.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0053.TXT2008]
As days went by, the fierceness of the conflict only increased.
