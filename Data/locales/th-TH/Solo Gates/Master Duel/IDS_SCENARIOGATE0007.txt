[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TITLE1001]
Ruin and Demise
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1001]
When a world is about to perish,
it is seen on its way by two beings.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1002]
The first is a red being that envelops
in golden light and draws the curtain on the world.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1003]
She is an androgynous goddess
garbed in red robes.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1004]
She is a god with no believers.
That's only natural.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1005]
A world visited by the goddess “Ruin”
is on the path to destruction.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1006]
Any intelligent life
that would believe in and worship that God
would soon cease to exist.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1007]
The second is a black being that envelops the world
in blue flames which pulverizes the earth.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1008]
A towering, omnipotent demon king
adorned in black armor.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1009]
He is a king with no vassals.
That's only natural.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1010]
A world visited by the demon king “Demise”
will soon meet its end.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT1011]
Those who would respect and serve that king
would soon have their very lives destroyed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2001]
The red being, “Ruin, Queen of Oblivion,”
brings to the world a reversal of time.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2002]
When evil has spread throughout civilization,
and when the world's end is in sight,
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2003]
the red being
returns the world to a state
before that cause of evil occurred.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2004]
There are cases in which
this reversal may restore the world
to a primordial state full of intelligent life.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2005]
In this way, the world is reborn
with hopes that life will transpire
on the correct path.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2006]
For those living in the current world,
this would be synonymous with “extinction.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2007]
The process of rewinding the world to
promote the proper development of life.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2008]
What if the world continually takes a wrong path
no matter how many times it restarts?
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2009]
The black being, “Demise, King of Armageddon”,
brings about the true end of the world.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2010]
Its formidable power crushes the world's
very foundations and eradicates all living things.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2011]
There remains no trace that the world was there,
only absolute nothingness.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2012]
The “Queen of Oblivion” and “King of Armageddon”.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2013]
What lies in the heart of a being
who witnessed the destruction of the world
countless times over eons?
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0007.TXT2014]
That remains unknown
to those who disappear.
