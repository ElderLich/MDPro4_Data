[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TITLE1001]
The Warriors of the Six Elemental Lords
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1001]
The world is comprised of six elements:
earth, water, fire, wind,
light, and darkness.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1002]
The elements were brought to earth by six “Elemental Lords.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1003]
The world is at peace as long as
the six elements are in equilibrium.
However, when that balance is disturbed,
the “Elemental Lords” appear.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1004]
When the “Elemental Lords” physical forms have increased
in density enough to be seen by humans, disaster occurs.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1005]
The “Elemental Lords” have appeared numerous times,
causing natural disasters throughout the world.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1006]
The ancients built temples
to calm these recurring
natural disasters.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1007]
People prayed continually.  
But the “Elemental Lords” did not possess the capacity
to answer those prayers.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1008]
However, the people's desperate prayers would be answered
miraculously after hundreds of years.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1009]
Another power that had
nothing to do with the “Elemental Lords”
may have taken pity on them.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1010]
The “Elemental Lords” chose a handful of humans.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT1011]
They were called the “Elementsaber.”
They served the “Elemental Lords”
and maintained the order of the elements.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT2001]
The “Elementsaber”
now protect the temples, which pilgrims no longer visit.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT2002]
And day to day, they manage the elements that
comprise the world to not flare out of control.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT2003]
Let's say, for example, the element of fire flares up.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT2004]
The “Elementsaber” would
enhance the power of the water element.
It would rain throughout the land, extinguishing the flames.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT2005]
When one element gets out of hand,
the forces of the opposite element are increased,
restoring balance.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT2006]
The “Elementsabers”
inherit only a fraction of the “Elemental Lords” authority
and cannot exercise all their power.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT2007]
Nor should that power
make them feel as if
they are gods.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT2008]
They use their ability to command, train, and do right.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0006.TXT2009]
Training never ends
for the “Elementsabers.”
