[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TITLE1001]
Military Revolution -Mecha Phantom Beasts-
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1001]
The development of new technologies
will greatly transforms the world as we know it.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1002]
It is in the military industry that this is most evident.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1003]
This time let's explore the rapid development and evolution
of the military aircraft known as “Mecha Phantom Beasts.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1004]
By land, by sea and by air, 
various military crafts have proved effective over time.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1005]
The most advanced technology of the time is generously incorporated, 
leading to further technological innovation.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1006]
Dominating the skies was a top strategical priority.
 Countless engineers have poured their blood, sweat, and tears
 into improving their aerial prowess through trial and error.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1007]
A group of engineers turned their sights to incorporating quantum technology
 into their research on creating new methods of defence and offence in war,
 and aimed to invent a new, innovative decoy system.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1008]
Once they completed a quantum generator that could create decoys
 that were extraordinarily close to the real thing,
 the first prototype they tested it on was "Raiten."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1009]
Its very entity, let alone its appearance,
are misinterpreted by enemy instrumentation 
thanks to its decoy function,
assuring a high survival rate for the craft.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1010]
Mecha Phantom Beast, machines made in the image of animals,
 soon became the standard in aerial warfare.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1011]
The success of “Mecha Phantom Beast Raiten”
birthed other masterpieces such as 
“Mecha Phantom Beast Tetherwolf”
and “Mecha Phantom Beast Megaraptor.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1012]
Then a large transport aircraft,
“Mecha Phantom Beast Dracossack” was developed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT1013]
Known as the first-generation aircraft,
 they are still flown today.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2001]
Decoys capable of misidentifying 
as actual machines were completed,
and aircraft equipped with this technology performed 
even better than expected.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2002]
Eventually evolving past mere decoys,
the search for new tactics settled upon the use of illusions,
which were lumps of quantum energy.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2003]
The second generation aimed to diversify
the usage of decoy tactics:
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2004]
“Mecha Phantom Beast Turtletracer” and
“Mecha Phantom Beast Concoruda.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2005]
Utilizing previously disposable decoys,
various aircraft with new designs were developed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2006]
In the third generation,
a high output craft was developed 
that operated on the energy of decoys:
“Mecha Phantom Beast Kalgriffin.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2007]
And beyond the mere atmosphere,
a craft for expansion into outer space
was developed: “Mecha Phantom Beast O-Lion.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2008]
Finally, the final stage of “Mecha Phantom Beast” development,
flagship aircraft carrier
“Phantom Fortress Enterblathnir” was completed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2009]
Thanks to the so-called Aerial Fortress “Enterblathnir,”
the skies of the world
became dominated by the “Mecha Phantom Beasts.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2010]
However, technologies progress day by day.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2011]
Capable of instantly generating up to three decoys at a time,
the state-of-the-art “Mecha Phantom Beast Auroradon”
has just been put into service.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2012]
New “Mecha Phantom Beasts”
will surely continue to be developed by engineers.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0018.TXT2013]
Their impact on the course of history
will likely soon be upon us.
