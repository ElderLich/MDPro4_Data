[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TITLE1001]
Enforcers of Justice
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1001]
Sword in hand.
Clad in armor.
Body adorned in robes.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1002]
All shine brightly like platinum,
the heroic platoon's display of power.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1003]
They're known as the “Lightsworn.”
From whence did they come, and for what do they fight?
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1004]
The wishes of those praying for peace,
the cries of those suffering under tyranny,
the innocent tears of children—
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1005]
They appear suddenly,
an army of light breaking through 
the barrier of space and time.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1006]
To each they aid, their justice embodies a different form.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1007]
Some see a knight in shining armor.
Others see a graceful wizard.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1008]
Still others see a divine angel,
or a dragon so great it eclipses the mountain 
with a single wing,
they claim.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1009]
Regardless of the form the enforcers take,
their mission is unchanging.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1010]
After annihilating the wicked,
they vanish without a trace.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1011]
They live in the radiant
“Realm of Light.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT1012]
In a world existing in another plane of space and time,
they continually train in preparation for dispensing justice.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT2001]
Though the “Lightsworn” possess ultimate power,
sometimes even for them
the evils that appear are too great.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT2002]
When the enemy is too fierce,
have they no choice but to give up on justice?
That is not an option.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT2003]
Deep within the “Realm of Light”
lies a tightly sealed treasure chest.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT2004]
When a mighty enemy appears,
the treasure box's heavy lid opens.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT2005]
Neatly folded robes of black await a warrior of justice.
Possessing the power of darkness, therein slumbers
the “Twilight Cloth.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT2006]
When adorned by a “Lightsworn,”
that warrior of justice is imbued with the powers
of both light and darkness.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT2007]
They who wear the “Twilight Cloth”
show no mercy to the forces of evil.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT2008]
Joining forces with the black “Punishment Dragon,”
those warriors will not relent in their onslaught
until all wickedness is vanquished from the world.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT3001]
The “Lightsworn”
cannot inhabit this world for long.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT3002]
This is because they require
enormous magical power in order to maintain their forms
while in this world.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT3003]
Hence, as soon as the evil is destroyed,
they vanish in rays
back to the “Realm of Light.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT3004]
Transcending time and space,
their battle for justice never ceases.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT3005]
Some have honed their skills in the “Realm of Light”
to receive new roles as enforcers of justice.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT3006]
The “Lightsworn” have great hopes
that the people's hearts will be at peace.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT3007]
Hopefully one day
the world won't need such
enforcers of justice.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0017.TXT3008]
Until that day comes,
they will continue to dispense justice
wherever it is called for.
