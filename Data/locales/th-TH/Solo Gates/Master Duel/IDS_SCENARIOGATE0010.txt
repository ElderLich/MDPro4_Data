[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TITLE1001]
The Glory of the Gladiator Beasts
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1001]
In ancient times, a great empire ruled the world.
The city was prosperous, and its inhabitants lived in peace.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1002]
War was a thing of faraway lands,
and the aristocrats, unknowing of the danger,
decided to build a great amphitheater, the "Colosseum."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1003]
Therein “Gladiator Beasts”
fought to the death for sport.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1004]
Tournaments were held day and night
to satisfy the latent bloodlust of the aristocrats.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1005]
Long live the empire!　
Salute the dying heroes!
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1006]
At the blast of a horn, a fierce battle would begin,
in which the beasts would fight with claws, fangs,
and any weapons available.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1007]
At the “Colosseum,” those who captivated the crowds
were lavished with praise.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1008]
This spurred on the “Gladiator Beasts,”
who risked great peril
for the spectators' cheers.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1009]
The empire enjoyed the very height of prosperity,
but a small spark would set it on the path to destruction.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1010]
And with the decline of the empire,
the frequency of fighting tournaments decreased.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1011]
The aristocrats sealed the “Colosseum” for eternity,
for fear that the “Gladiator Beasts,”
who'd lost their battleground, might revolt.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1012]
Many years passed since
the fall of the empire, and the “Colosseum”
decayed over time.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1013]
But one day,
the ground with faded bloodstains shined,
and the “Gladiator Beasts” appeared.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1014]
Among the seats long empty of spectators,
the starting horn once again echoed.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1015]
Long live the empire!　
Salute the dying heroes!
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1016]
Inside the “Colosseum,”
framed by that decaying city,
the “Gladiator Beasts” fought once again.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT1017]
And in jubilation of their heroic travails,
the cheers of the aristocrats
could once more be heard.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT2001]
The “Gladiator Beasts” enacted various contests
as if in response to the voices of invisible spectators.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT2002]
One-on-one combat, platoon battles, sieges, naval battles...
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT2003]
And when their excitement peaked, 
the seal that bound the bravest of 
the “Gladiator Beasts” was broken.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT2004]
In close-combat platoon battles, 
they were the "Gladiator Beasts", 
beasts proud of their iron physiques.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT2005]
In the large-scale sieges, 
they were bird “Gladiator Beasts” 
most active in raiding parties and airal assault.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT2006]
In the naval battles at the “Colosseum,”
they were the “Gladiator Beasts” of fish and sea serpent form,
who drew their prey down into the dark waters.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT2007]
And the deadly weapons they'd wielded, 
“Battle Machines" and “Chariots,” were revived.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT2008]
The “Colosseum” regained its glory
due to the brave heroics
of the “Gladiator Beasts.”
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0010.TXT2009]
When the fervor of their contests reached an epic crescendo,
the gates of the “Colosseum” were opened,
and their terrifying death march commenced.
