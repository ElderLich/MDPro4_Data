Worshippers of the Sacred Phoenix
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT1001]
"<card mrk='6236' get='name'/>."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT1002]
A god enrobed in flames,
who came to the mortal realm blazing so brightly
it could have been mistaken 
for the second coming of the sun.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT1003]
It was worshiped and revered in a certain land
where dry winds billowed 
and desert stretched in all directions.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT1004]
Each generation had a priest with powers
to embody this deity.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT1005]
With the power of their strong soul,
they would offer the prayers and hopes 
brought forth by their ritual
to manifest Nephthys and start many miracles.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT1006]
Those who were especially devout to the Phoenix God
had built statues of the priests' dynasty 
and enshrined them in a particular part of the desert 
that was declared sacred land.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT1007]
They thanked the god for watching over them
and toiled every day with its blessings
to build their homeland bigger and better.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2001]
But one day, disaster struck
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2002]
without a single warning
when a tremendous entity arrived in their land.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2003]
The soldiers armed themselves for battle immediately.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2004]
They had faith that all evil would bow to their god,
and the standing priest at the time 
manifested the Phoenix God once again.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2005]
Little did they know, the bringer of ruin
they faced had even greater power.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2006]
The Phoenix God's strength was insufficient,
and the desert land was left defenseless 
against this calamity.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2007]
The only hope the people had to protect their home
was a young girl who many believed
had the potential to become the next priest.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2008]
When they tried to appeal to their god again,
there were no offerings, no music, no dancing—
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2009]
only the whispers of the girl as she
devoted herself to prayer while her homeland
burned and crumbled around her.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2010]
The god heard her genuine wish and
answered to the cries of her heart.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2011]
In the girl's soul dwelled a power
so divine and unheard of in its intensity
that she was able to awaken 
"<card mrk='14016' get='name'/>."
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2012]
The crimson Phoenix God descended with 
its flames of gold, and with one swoop of its wings, 
the fires ripping through the land disappeared.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2013]
Once the catastrophe had settled,
the god became a statue once more.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2014]
A few months later...
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2015]
Peace had returned to the people's homeland and
they were once again holding a ritual 
in honor of the great Nephthys.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2016]
Among the worshipers was the girl
who had been the sole ray of hope in the disaster.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2017]
She seemed more mature now, and carried the air of
one who was determined to be ready 
for whenever the next tragedy struck.
[IDS_SCENARIOGATE0028.TXT2018]
She clasped her hands together
and prayed for another day of peace and wellness.
